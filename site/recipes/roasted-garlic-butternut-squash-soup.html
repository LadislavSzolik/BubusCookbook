Roasted Garlic Butternut Squash Soup

A one-tray soup of roasted butternut squash, peppers, carrots, tomatoes and garlic, blended smooth and served with chili oil, coconut milk and sourdough.

Ingredients

For the soup

  • 1 butternut squash, halved with seeds removed
  • 1 red onion, halved
  • 1 red bell pepper
  • 4 carrots, peeled
  • 2 heads garlic
  • 2–3 cups cherry tomatoes
  • 2–4 tbsp avocado or olive oil
  • 1 tsp each paprika, pepper and thyme
  • Salt, to taste
  • 2 cups vegetable broth, plus more if needed

To serve

  • Chili oil
  • Coconut milk, for drizzling
  • Fresh herbs
  • Sourdough

Directions

  1. Preheat the oven to 425°F (220°C). Place the squash cut-side up on a baking tray and add the onion halves into the squash cavities. Place the bell pepper, carrots, garlic and cherry tomatoes on the tray.
  2. Drizzle everything with oil and season with salt, paprika, pepper and thyme. Try to rub the seasoning into the squash.
  3. Roast for 50–60 minutes, until everything is soft and caramelized.
  4. Scoop the squash out of the skin and add everything from the tray to a blender, including the roasted garlic (remove its skin) and the juices.
  5. Add the vegetable broth and blend until smooth.
  6. Warm the soup up however you like and adjust the seasonings as you see fit.
  7. Serve with chili oil, a drizzle of coconut milk, fresh herbs and some sourdough.

Notes

Minimal effort and just one tray.
