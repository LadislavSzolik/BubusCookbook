# Chicken Pot Pie Soup

![Chicken Pot Pie Soup](../images/chicken-pot-pie-soup.jpg)

*A creamy one-pot soup of shredded chicken thighs, potatoes, carrots, celery and peas in a coconut cream or heavy cream broth, seasoned with sage and Italian herbs.*

## Ingredients

### For the soup

- 1–2 lb boneless, skinless chicken thighs
- 2 tbsp cooking oil (the author used tallow, olive oil works too)
- 1 tsp garlic powder, divided
- 1 tsp paprika
- 1 medium yellow onion, diced
- 3–4 cloves fresh garlic, minced
- 1 cup frozen mixed vegetables (carrots, peas and corn), plus extra chopped carrots if you like
- 2–3 celery sticks, chopped
- 1 tsp Italian seasoning
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- 1 tsp dried sage
- Salt, to taste
- 3 tbsp cornstarch
- 5 cups chicken broth
- 2 medium yellow potatoes, diced
- 1 can coconut cream (about 13.5 oz), or 1–1½ cups heavy cream

## Method

1. Season the chicken thighs with paprika, garlic powder and salt. Heat the oil in a Dutch oven over medium-high heat and sear the chicken for 4–5 minutes per side. Remove, cool, shred and set aside.
2. In the same pot, sauté the onion for 2–3 minutes. Add the garlic, frozen vegetables, extra carrots and celery. Cook for another 2–3 minutes.
3. Add the Italian seasoning, garlic powder, sage and salt. Stir in the cornstarch and cook for 1 minute.
4. Pour in the chicken broth and simmer for 5–7 minutes, until the vegetables begin to soften. Add the potatoes and cook another 10–15 minutes, until tender.
5. Whisk the coconut cream (or heavy cream) with a few tablespoons of hot broth until smooth. Stir it into the soup.
6. Add the shredded chicken back in, stir and simmer for a few minutes. Taste and adjust the seasoning as needed.
