# Creamy Coconut Lime Chicken

![Creamy Coconut Lime Chicken](../images/creamy-coconut-lime-chicken.jpg)

*Golden-seared chicken simmered in a silky coconut sauce, finished with fresh lime and coriander.*

## Ingredients

- 6–8 chicken thighs, or 4–5 breasts
- Paprika
- 1 large shallot
- 1 green chilli
- 1 tbsp minced garlic
- 1 tbsp minced ginger
- 200 ml chicken stock
- 400 ml coconut milk
- 160 ml coconut cream
- 1 tbsp dark soy sauce
- 1 tbsp fish sauce
- Coriander
- Lime, zest and juice
- Olive oil, 1–2 tbsp
- Salt and pepper

## Method

1. Heat 1–2 tbsp olive oil in a large pan. Add the chicken and season with salt, pepper and paprika. Cook on both sides until golden, then remove.
2. Add the shallot and chilli and cook for 3–5 minutes over low heat. Stir through the ginger, garlic and lime zest and heat for 1–2 minutes.
3. Pour in the stock, coconut milk and coconut cream. Keep a little cream back to top the chicken at the end.
4. Mix in the soy sauce, return the chicken to the pan, cover and cook for 10–15 minutes over low heat, until the chicken is cooked through and the sauce has thickened.
5. Serve with the fish sauce, lime juice, dollops of coconut cream, coriander and lime slices.
