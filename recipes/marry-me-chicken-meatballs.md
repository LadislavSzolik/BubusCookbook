# Marry Me Chicken Meatballs with Garlic Mashed Potatoes & Sautéed Lemon Asparagus

![Marry Me Chicken Meatballs with Garlic Mashed Potatoes & Sautéed Lemon Asparagus](../images/marry-me-chicken-meatballs.jpg)

*Italian seasoned chicken meatballs tossed in a creamy garlic-parmesan sauce with sun-dried tomatoes and fresh basil, served with garlic mashed potatoes and sautéed lemon asparagus.*

## Ingredients

### Chicken meatballs

- 1 lb ground chicken
- ¼ cup shredded parmesan
- ¼ cup panko breadcrumbs
- ½ white onion, finely diced
- 2 garlic cloves, minced
- 1 egg
- 1 tbsp Italian seasoning
- ½ tsp salt
- ¼ tsp black pepper

### Marry me sauce

- Olive oil, a small splash
- ¼ cup white onion, finely diced
- ½ cup jarred sun-dried tomatoes in oil, drained and chopped
- 1 tbsp tomato paste
- 3 garlic cloves, minced
- 1½ cups chicken broth
- ¾ cup heavy cream
- ½ cup shredded parmesan
- 1 tsp Italian seasoning
- ¼–½ tsp red pepper flakes, depending on spice preference

### To serve

- Fresh basil, thinly sliced

### Garlic mashed potatoes

- 2 lb Yukon gold potatoes, peeled and diced
- 3 garlic cloves, minced
- ¼ cup butter
- ¾ cup milk of choice
- ¼ tsp + ⅛ tsp salt (plus extra for boiling the potatoes)
- Cracked black pepper, to taste

### Sautéed lemon asparagus

- 1 bundle of asparagus
- Olive oil
- Salt
- Cracked black pepper
- Lemon zest

## Method

### Chicken meatballs and sauce

1. Heat the oven to 400°F (200°C) and grease a baking dish with cooking spray.
2. Combine the meatball ingredients in a large bowl. Roll the mixture into 1-inch (2.5 cm) balls and place them in the baking dish. (You can also brown the meatballs in a skillet for a few minutes per side before baking.)
3. Bake the meatballs for 15–20 minutes.
4. While the meatballs bake, heat a splash of olive oil in a large skillet over medium heat. Cook the diced onion for 2–3 minutes until tender. Add the sun-dried tomatoes and garlic, stir in the tomato paste and cook for a few minutes.
5. Add the broth, cream, parmesan, Italian seasoning and red pepper flakes. Simmer over medium-high heat for 5–7 minutes, stirring occasionally, until combined and slightly thickened. The sauce may look thin, but it thickens as it cools.
6. Add the cooked meatballs to the skillet and toss to coat. Garnish with basil and serve.

### Garlic mashed potatoes

1. Add the potatoes to a pot of cold water with a handful of salt. Cover and bring to a boil. Boil for 15–20 minutes or until the potatoes are tender.
2. Heat the butter, milk and minced garlic together in a small pot over medium-low until the butter melts and it's warm throughout.
3. Transfer the cooked potatoes to a large bowl and mash with a potato masher. Add the milk and butter mixture, salt and cracked pepper to taste. Continue mashing and mixing until smooth. (You can also use a handheld mixer to whip the potatoes.)
4. Taste and adjust the seasonings, if needed.

### Sautéed lemon asparagus

1. Add a splash of olive oil to a large skillet and heat over medium heat. Once hot, add the asparagus and sauté for 5–8 minutes or until just tender.
2. Generously season with salt and pepper. Sprinkle lemon zest over the top and toss. (Sometimes we'll add a squeeze of lemon too!)

## Notes

The meatballs and sauce follow the author's blog recipe (4 servings, 20 minutes prep and 25 minutes cook). The potatoes and asparagus come from the Instagram caption.

**Substitutions:** Ground turkey or beef works in place of the chicken. For a dairy-free version, use dairy-free cream and non-dairy parmesan.

**Storage:** Refrigerate the meatballs and sauce together in an airtight container for 3–4 days. Reheat in a covered skillet over medium-low heat until heated through.
